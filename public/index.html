The problem

Digital education still delivers limited evidence of knowledge.

Many platforms track screen time and completion without proving what was actually learned. Certificates remain isolated, reputation is hard to carry, and records depend on centralized infrastructure.

Low verifiability

Outcomes are not easily audited outside the platform.

Fragmented certification

Credentials can be falsified or fail to follow learners across ecosystems.

Limited portability

Educational reputation rarely travels between communities and institutions.

Centralized incentives

Rewards are managed by siloed systems, not by transparent infrastructure.

The Academy solution

An infrastructure for learning, validation and digital ownership.

Academy pairs modern learning experiences with an on-chain validation layer. Content stays fast and accessible while meaningful events generate verifiable records, incentives and certifications.

Structured learning

Short lessons, micro-quizzes and progressive journeys with smart recommendations.

Proof-of-Knowledge

Qualified learning actions generate verifiable evidence of progress and skills.

Programmable rewards

Qualified actions can earn $Neurons under controlled issuance rules.

Verifiable certification

Soulbound credentials linked to the learner’s digital identity.

Community and governance

$Neurons enables participation, benefits, delegation and voting.

Hybrid architecture

Off-chain performance with on-chain verification where it matters.

Security by design

Proof-of-Knowledge

Rewards based on verifiable learning.

The mechanism converts eligible learning actions into rewards under policies that enforce daily limits, epoch budgets and quality criteria.

Quality factors

Participation, answer quality, difficulty, consistency and completion.

Defined guardrails

Daily caps, activity cooldowns, epoch budgets and decaying issuance.

Robust verification

Verifiable signatures, claim validation and anti-sybil safeguards.

Current status

A functional foundation in evolution.

Academy already has documented architecture, defined on-chain/off-chain boundaries, abstracted persistence and responsive development flows. The prototype serves as a validation environment while sensitive contract and financial workflows remain staged.

Documented: architecture and boundary design.
Validated: persistence, progress and integrity flows.
Tested: automated quality and end-to-end tests.
Controlled: sensitive contract and financial flows in later phases.

Roadmap

Planned evolution in controlled phases.

Phase 1

Core learner journey, course management, quizzes, progress and persistence.

Phase 2

$Neurons token, KnowledgeMinter, PaymentGateway, CertificateSBT and wallet integration.

Phase 3

Testnet deployment, audits, monitoring, relayer, KMS and anti-sybil policies.

Phase 4

Marketplace, governance, premium communities, white-label and multi-chain expansion.

Long-term vision

A reputation layer built on knowledge.

Academy aims to make educational history more verifiable, portable and useful. Over time, learners can build a knowledge identity that unlocks new opportunities and economic participation.
