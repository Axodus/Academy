# Academy Reward Classes

Status: Draft v0.1

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the reward classes of the Axodus Academy.

The Academy operates with multiple reward layers to ensure:
- sustainability
- anti-farming protection
- progressive qualification
- economic alignment
- ecosystem stability

---

# 2. Core Philosophy

Not every reward represents liquid economic participation.

The Academy separates:
- educational progression value
- ecosystem participation value
- liquid economic value

This separation protects the ecosystem from:
- inflation
- farming abuse
- speculative extraction
- unsustainable emissions

---

# 3. Reward Classes

The Academy currently defines two primary reward classes.

---

# Class A — Locked Educational Rewards

Origin:
- free courses
- onboarding tracks
- public learning systems

Format:
- locked $NEURONS
- internal educational balance
- non-transferable ecosystem participation

---

## Characteristics

Locked rewards:
- cannot be withdrawn
- cannot be transferred
- cannot be swapped
- cannot leave ecosystem control

Locked rewards may:
- participate in governance
- purchase internal services
- acquire NFTs
- unlock products
- purchase vouchers
- access marketplace utilities

---

## Purpose

Locked rewards exist to:
- encourage progression
- reduce farming abuse
- create educational utility
- onboard participants safely

---

# Class B — Liquid Participation Rewards

Origin:
- paid courses
- premium educational tracks
- advanced certifications
- qualified ecosystem contribution

Format:
- liquid $NEURONS
- direct wallet distribution

---

## Characteristics

Liquid rewards:
- are transferable
- may enter open markets
- may participate in staking
- may be used economically

---

## Purpose

Liquid rewards exist to:
- reward committed participants
- encourage economic alignment
- strengthen ecosystem contribution
- reward qualified participation

---

# 4. Reward Distribution Logic

The PoKMinter may determine:
- reward amount
- reward class
- release timing
- liquidity eligibility
- trust modifiers

---

# 5. Locked Reward Infrastructure

Locked rewards may be managed through:
- PoKMinter
- LockedNeuronsVault
- educational accounting systems

The system must prevent:
- unauthorized transfers
- liquidity bypass
- swap abuse
- external extraction

---

# 6. Governance Relationship

The Governance DAO maintains authority over:
- emission budgets
- reward policies
- macroeconomic parameters
- sustainability controls

The Academy maintains operational authority over:
- reward qualification
- educational progression
- distribution logic
- trust requirements

---

# 7. Trust and Liquidity

Liquidity access may depend on:
- educational maturity
- contribution quality
- certifications
- ecosystem trust
- operational progression

The ecosystem may progressively unlock economic rights.

---

# 8. Anti-Inflation Design

The reward architecture exists to:
- reduce market pressure
- prevent exploitative extraction
- maintain long-term sustainability
- align incentives with contribution

---

# 9. Long-Term Vision

The reward system exists to transform:
- learning
into
- qualified ecosystem participation.

    The Academy rewards contribution, not passive extraction.
