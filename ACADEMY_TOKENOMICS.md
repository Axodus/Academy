# Academy Tokenomics

---

# Core Principle

$NEURONS enters circulation through validated cognitive contribution.

The Academy is the constitutional gateway for organic token distribution.

---

# Distribution Flow

Governance DAO
→ Treasury Allocation
→ PoKMinter Budget
→ Educational Validation
→ Reward Distribution

---

# Distribution Goals

The system must:
- avoid inflationary abuse
- prevent farming
- reward real participation
- maintain sustainability

---

# Potential Reward Sources

- course completion
- quizzes
- certifications
- operational contribution
- governance participation
- educational contribution
- ecosystem engagement

---

# Sustainability Controls

Potential mechanisms:
- epoch budgets
- daily caps
- cooldowns
- score decay
- anti-sybil systems
- governance overrides
