# Strategic Mission

Create the sovereign educational infrastructure of Axodus.

The Academy will:
- onboard users
- educate contributors
- qualify operators
- distribute $NEURONS meritocratically
- strengthen governance quality
- create ecosystem workforce

Distribute $NEURONS through:
- verified learning
- certifications
- operational contribution
- participation quality

Transform the Academy into:

- a contributor formation engine
