01 / The gap

Completion is not the same as understanding.

Digital education is good at tracking access and completion. It is much less effective at proving what a learner actually understood, what they can apply and whether that history can travel with them.

Academy is designed to make learning evidence more useful beyond a single platform.

01

Limited evidence

Screen time and completion rarely show the quality of knowledge acquired.

02

Fragmented credentials

Certificates remain isolated, difficult to verify and easy to misrepresent.

03

Closed reputation

Progress and trust usually stay locked inside a centralized database.

04

Weak incentives

Learning, contribution and community participation are rarely connected.

02 / The Academy

A learning layer built for evidence, identity and participation.

Academy combines a fast learning experience with a validation layer that can turn meaningful educational events into portable records, credentials and controlled incentives.

More than a course platform.

Academy treats learning as an infrastructure problem: how to create credible evidence, protect identity, align incentives and make achievements useful across communities.

01

Knowledge as an asset

Learning events are structured so other applications can verify and use them.

02

Hybrid by design

Fast off-chain experiences are combined with on-chain verification where it adds value.

03

Modular architecture

06

Utility over speculation

05 / Proof-of-Knowledge

09 / Trust layer

Security is part of the product lifecycle.

From short-lived authentication to smart-contract controls and operational monitoring, the platform is designed to fail closed while it moves toward production maturity.

01

Application controls

Role-based access, short-lived JWTs, rate limiting and SHA-256 integrity checks.

02

Contract controls

Pausing, reentrancy protection, EIP-712 signatures and configurable issuance limits.

03

Operational readiness

Idempotent processing, retries with backoff, automated testing and external audit planned before production.

13 / Roadmap

Progress through deliberate phases.

The roadmap prioritizes a usable product first, then introduces on-chain operations under progressively stronger controls.

Phase 01

Product foundation

Learner journey, courses, quizzes, progress, persistence and validation.

Phase 02

On-chain infrastructure

$Neurons, KnowledgeMinter, PaymentGateway, CertificateSBT and wallet integration.

Phase 03

Security & operations

Testnet, audits, monitoring, relayer, KMS and anti-sybil policies.

Phase 04

Expansion

Marketplace, governance, premium communities, institutions and multi-chain growth.
