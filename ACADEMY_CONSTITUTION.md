# Axodus Academy Constitution

Version: Draft v0.1  
Status: Foundational Constitutional Draft  
Scope: Axodus CORE Ecosystem  
Governance Model: Federated Constitutional Governance

---

# 1. Purpose

The Axodus Academy exists as the sovereign cognitive infrastructure of the Axodus ecosystem.

Its purpose is to:
- educate participants
- qualify operational capability
- align ecosystem philosophy
- distribute economic participation meritocratically
- strengthen governance quality
- reduce systemic risk
- create sustainable ecosystem contributors

The Academy is not merely a learning platform.

The Academy is a constitutional infrastructure layer responsible for transforming participants into capable sovereign actors within the Axodus Federation.

---

# 2. Constitutional Identity

The Academy is the official sovereign infrastructure responsible for:
- Proof of Knowledge (PoK)
- ecosystem onboarding
- operational qualification
- meritocratic participation distribution
- educational reputation
- ecosystem trust progression

The Academy operates as:
- a cognitive layer
- a merit validation system
- a governance qualification primitive
- an economic participation gateway

---

# 3. Constitutional Principles

The Academy shall operate under the following constitutional principles:

## 3.1 Meritocracy

Economic participation shall be distributed according to validated contribution, knowledge and operational maturity.

---

## 3.2 Sustainability

The Academy shall never operate under uncontrolled inflationary mechanics.

Token distribution must remain sustainable, governed and economically responsible.

---

## 3.3 Sovereignty

Participants are expected to evolve toward operational sovereignty, responsibility and governance awareness.

---

## 3.4 Security

Security education is considered a constitutional obligation.

Operational ignorance represents systemic risk.

---

## 3.5 Transparency

Reward mechanics, scoring systems and progression rules must remain auditable and transparent.

---

## 3.6 Anti-Exploitation

The Academy must actively resist:
- sybil behavior
- farming abuse
- fake engagement
- artificial progression
- malicious automation

---

## 3.7 Educational Integrity

Certifications, scores and qualifications must preserve ecosystem trust and cannot become meaningless inflationary credentials.

---

- constitutional amendments

The Academy maintains operational autonomy regarding:
- Proof of Knowledge validation

- educational progression

# 5. The Role of the Academy in Token Distribution

The Academy is the official constitutional gateway for organic distribution of the $NEURONS token.

The Academy itself does not create tokens.

The Governance DAO allocates controlled emission budgets to the Proof of Knowledge system.

The PoK infrastructure distributes tokens according to:
- verified learning
- operational progression
- validated contribution
- ecosystem participation
- educational merit

This mechanism exists to ensure:
- sustainable circulation
- meritocratic participation
- organic ecosystem growth
- resistance to speculative concentration

---

# 6. Proof of Knowledge (PoK)

Proof of Knowledge is the constitutional validation system responsible for:
- educational verification
- merit validation
- reward qualification
- operational progression
- trust generation

PoK is not a faucet.

PoK is an ecosystem qualification engine.

---

# 7. PoKMinter Constitutional Role

The PoKMinter contract acts as the operational distribution engine of the Academy.

Its responsibilities include:
- receiving DAO-approved reward budgets
- validating reward claims
- enforcing emission rules
- applying cooldowns and caps
- distributing $NEURONS
- preventing abuse
- recording participation metrics

The PoKMinter must never exceed governance-approved limits.

- learning
- certification
