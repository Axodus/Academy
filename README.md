# Axodus Learn-to-Win 🎓✨

**Axodus Learn-to-Win** is the first **on-chain learning platform** powered by **Proof-of-Knowledge (PoK)** and the **$NEURONS token**.  
Here, learning is more than gaining knowledge — it’s a way to **earn, invest, and participate in governance**.

---

## 🚀 What is Learn-to-Win?

Traditional e-learning platforms reward only the platform.  
Axodus flips the model: **students are rewarded for learning**.  
By studying, passing quizzes, and completing certifications, students **mine $NEURONS (NRS)**, a native token with real utility inside and outside the ecosystem.

This is **Learn-to-Win**:  
- Knowledge → Rewards → Empowerment → Community Growth.  

---

## 🧠 Proof-of-Knowledge (PoK)

**Proof-of-Knowledge** is a novel mechanism where **learning activities generate tokens**.  
Instead of expensive mining rigs or energy waste, the **proof is your effort and progress**.  

Examples of PoK rewards:  
- 📘 Attending a class  
- 📝 Completing a quiz  
- 🔥 Maintaining a daily streak  
- 🎓 Passing the final certification (minted as a **Soulbound NFT Certificate**)  

Every interaction contributes to both your education **and** the growth of the ecosystem.
