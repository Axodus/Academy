# Academy Progress Engine

Status: Draft v0.1

---

# 1. Purpose

The Progress Engine defines how participants evolve inside the Axodus Academy.

Its responsibilities include:
- progression
- trust evolution
- qualification
- unlock systems
- educational maturity
- operational readiness
- ecosystem participation

The Progress Engine transforms users from passive learners into sovereign ecosystem contributors.

---

# 2. Core Philosophy

Progression is not based solely on:
- time
- passive activity
- staking
- farming

Progression must prioritize:
- understanding
- operational capability
- contribution
- consistency
- ecosystem alignment
- responsible participation

---

# 3. Progression Layers

The Academy operates through progressive maturity layers.

---

## Level 0 — Visitor

Status:
- unauthenticated or new participant

Capabilities:
- browse public content
- access ecosystem introduction

Restrictions:
- no rewards
- no governance
- no advanced access

---

## Level 1 — Initiated

Requirements:
- wallet connection
- constitutional onboarding
- security introduction

Capabilities:
- access free learning tracks
- receive locked rewards
- basic participation

Rewards:
- Locked $NEURONS only

---

## Level 2 — Qualified

Requirements:
- completion of operational security modules
- passing minimum PoK validation

Capabilities:
- participate in intermediate courses
- access educational unlocks
- use internal marketplace utilities

Rewards:
- increased locked rewards

---

## Level 3 — Operator

Requirements:
- professional track progression
- operational validation
- certification milestones

Capabilities:
- ACS qualification eligibility
- advanced educational systems
- contributor pathways

Rewards:
- hybrid rewards possible

---

## Level 4 — Contributor

Requirements:
- ecosystem contribution
- advanced certifications
- operational consistency

Capabilities:
- governance qualification
- contributor reputation
- educational mentoring

Rewards:
- liquid $NEURONS access
- contribution-based incentives

---

## Level 5 — Sovereign

Requirements:
- long-term ecosystem contribution
- governance trust
- operational maturity
- ecosystem alignment

Capabilities:
- advanced governance participation
- federation roles
- ecosystem leadership pathways

Rewards:
- full ecosystem participation

---
